# Privacy Policy for Faciora

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

Faciora ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI-powered face analysis and personalized beauty guide app (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using Faciora, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

## 1. Face Data We Process

This section describes, in full, how Faciora handles the photo of your face that you submit for analysis.

**What face data we collect.** When you choose to take or upload a selfie, that single photo is the only face data Faciora collects. From it, our AI service produces a text-based analysis (observations about hair, brows, eyes, skin and lips) and a generated "after" image. Faciora does not create, extract, or store a separate biometric template, faceprint, or facial-recognition identifier from your photo, and the App is not used for identity verification or facial recognition of any kind.

**How it is used.** Your photo is used for a single purpose: to generate the personalized before/after report and beauty guide you requested inside the App, at the moment you request it.

**Who it is shared with.** Your photo is sent only to Google's Gemini API, the third-party AI service that performs the analysis and generates the "after" image on our behalf. It is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or any other third party, and it is never used for advertising.

**Where it is stored and for how long.** Faciora's own servers (Firebase Cloud Functions) do not store your photo. The photo is forwarded to Google's Gemini API in memory, as part of a single request, and the result is returned directly to your device in the same request; it is never written to a database, file storage, or log on our side. On Google's side, under Google's standard Gemini API data policy for billed API usage, your prompts and outputs are not used to train or improve Google's models, and are retained in Google's operational logs for a limited period (up to 55 days by default) before being marked for deletion — see [Google's Gemini API data policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/logs-policy) for details, as this is governed by Google, not by us.

**What happens on your device.** The photo you submitted, your analysis report, and the generated "after" image are saved only locally on your device (not uploaded to or backed up on our servers) so you can revisit them in your history. You can delete all of this at any time via **Profile → Delete my data**, or by uninstalling the App.

**Your control.** Submitting a photo for analysis is always your choice — the App requests camera/photo library access only when you start an analysis, and nothing is captured or sent without that action.

## 2. Other Information We Collect

### 2.1 Anonymous App Identifier
Faciora uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to generate a random, anonymous identifier for your device. This is used only to verify that requests to our analysis service come from a genuine copy of the App, and is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identity.

### 2.2 Purchase Information
If you buy diamonds (in-app credits used for analyses), the purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and, for entitlement management, RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive non-identifying purchase metadata (such as which product was bought) to grant you the correct number of diamonds.

### 2.3 Data Stored On Your Device
Your analysis history, saved guides, and diamond balance are stored locally on your device. This data is not automatically uploaded to our servers, and it is removed if you uninstall the App or use the "Delete my data" option in the App's Profile tab.

### 2.4 Information We Do Not Collect
We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact list. We do not access your device's photo library except for the specific photo you choose to analyze, share, or save, which requires your explicit permission.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Generate your personalized face analysis and before/after report
- Prevent abuse of our analysis service (via the anonymous app identifier)
- Process and fulfill in-app purchases
- Provide customer support when you contact us
- Improve and maintain the App

## 4. Third-Party Services

Faciora relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — for app distribution and payment processing
- **RevenueCat** — for managing in-app purchase entitlements ([https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy))
- **Firebase (Google)** — for anonymous authentication ([https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy))
- **Google Gemini API** — for processing your photo to generate your analysis report and "after" image ([https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/logs-policy](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/logs-policy))

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your photos for advertising purposes.

## 5. Data Retention

Your face photo and analysis results are not retained on Faciora's own servers at all — see Section 1 for the full explanation. Locally stored data (history, saved guides, diamond balance) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can:

- Delete your local analysis history and saved guides at any time via **Profile → Delete my data**
- Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data
- Contact us at sozensoftware@gmail.com to ask questions about your data or request deletion of any data held on our servers
